Query comparison
The sample contains 10,000 records and 140 repeated animal-ID occurrences.
Before adding the rescue index, each query examined 10,000 documents using the
existing _id index for ordering. Afterward, the rescue_lookup index reduced
documents examined to 18 (Water), 5 (Mountain), and 4 (Disaster).
Results and their order were identical before and after indexing.
The indexed plans still sorted the small matching result sets by _id.
Full counters, index sizes, and single-run explain timings are recorded in
query_comparison.json. The timings do not establish a general latency claim.
